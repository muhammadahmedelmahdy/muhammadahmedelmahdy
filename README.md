## About Me

I am a Machine Learning researcher currently pursuing my MSc in Informatics at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), specializing in Machine Learning and Computer Vision, and supported by the relAI Scholarship at the Konrad Zuse School of Excellence in Reliable AI.

Before starting my master's, I was already actively involved in research across multiple machine learning domains including reinforcement learning, explainable AI, federated learning, optimization, and medical AI systems. My earlier research experience included publications, large-scale experimental pipelines, and presenting research internationally at ACM SIGSPATIAL 2024 as an SRC Finalist.

My current work focuses on generative vision and modern representation learning systems, particularly:

- Diffusion Models
- Flow Matching
- Representation Learning
- Foundation Models
- Image & Video Generation
- 3D Generative Vision
- Vision-Language & Multimodal Models



My research interests center around building scalable and expressive foundation models capable of understanding and generating visual worlds.
